# Earth_Demo

Earth_Demo is a game built with Cocos that combines roguelike mechanics with auto-fire bullet-hell combat. The player controls movement to dodge bullet patterns while the character automatically fires at enemies. It is currently in early development.

## Story Background

In the future, a mysterious power transports all of humanity from Earth to strange new planets. Each person is given their own planet and spaceship. To find a way back to Earth, players must explore the galaxy, defend against space pirates, and gather resources to develop their planet and upgrade their spaceship.

## Planned Features

- Unique bullet patterns
- Life and research resource systems
- Planet development
- Spaceship exploration and resource gathering
- Defense against pirate attacks
