Earth_Demo is a game built witch Cocos,combining roguelike mechanics with ryhthm-based bullet hell.
It is currently in early development.
In th future,a mysterious power transports all of hummanity from Earth to strange new planets.
Each person is given their own planet and spaceship.
To find a way back to Earth, players must explore the galaxy, defend against space pirates, and gather resources to develop their planet and upgrade their spaceship.
Future palns include life and research resource systems that help players develop their planet.
Players can live on a planet, fly spaceships to search for resources, and defend against pirate attacks.
